When someone tells MYAI a family fact, such as who their father or mother
is, that fact is stored as a small logical statement, for example
parent(Arjun, Priya) meaning Arjun is a parent of Priya. Once enough facts
like this are known, MYAI can answer questions such as who someone's
grandfather is by chaining these statements together using logical rules,
rather than by guessing from text. This is called symbolic reasoning, and
it is more reliable than free-form text generation for this kind of
factual question.

MYAI also does basic arithmetic directly using a small math engine, so
questions like what is 12 plus 7 are answered by actually calculating the
result, not by predicting likely-looking text.
