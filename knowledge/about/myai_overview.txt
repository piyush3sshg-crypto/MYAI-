MYAI is a personal AI project built from scratch in Python, without relying
on large external AI frameworks for its core reasoning. It combines several
classic AI techniques in one place: symbolic logic (deduction, induction,
and abduction) for reasoning about facts, a knowledge graph for storing
relationships such as family trees, a simple planner for sequencing
actions, a hand-written neural network for classifying the intent behind a
message, and a small hand-written transformer-style language model for
open-ended conversation.

The project is meant as a learning and portfolio project: a way to
understand how these AI building blocks work by implementing them directly
rather than only calling a pre-built library. It runs entirely inside
Termux on a phone with no internet connection required for its core
features, though the /grow command can optionally fetch extra background
text from Wikipedia if the phone has an internet connection at the time.

MYAI can hold a short conversation, remember a few facts about the person
it's talking to (such as their name or who their family members are), do
basic arithmetic, and explain simple family relationships such as who is
whose grandparent using logical rules rather than guesswork.
