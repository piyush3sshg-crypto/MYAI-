There are seven days in a week: Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday,
Friday, Saturday, and Sunday. There are twelve months in a year, starting
with January and ending with December.

Water is made of two hydrogen atoms and one oxygen atom, written as the
chemical formula H2O. The speed of light in a vacuum is about 300,000
kilometers per second, which is the fastest known speed in the universe.
Gravity is the force that pulls objects toward each other; it is what
keeps people on the ground and keeps planets in orbit around the sun.

The world has seven continents: Asia, Africa, North America, South
America, Antarctica, Europe, and Australia. The capital of India is New
Delhi. A standard year has 365 days, and a leap year, which occurs every
four years, has 366 days.
